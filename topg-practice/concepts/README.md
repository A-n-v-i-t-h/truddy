## How to evaluate
1. Open each concept folder's `README.md` (rules practised, results tables, example charts with entry/SL/TP and outcome).
2. Mark each chart in `EVALUATION_SHEET.csv` (marking correct? entry valid? notes). 83 charts: 24 structure charts + up to 4 examples per timeframe per concept.
3. Every individual trade is in each folder's `trades.csv` if you want to spot-check any setup on TradingView (times are IST).

## Key findings (my reading of the results)
- **Confirmation matters.** Waiting for a lower-timeframe engulfing candle at the zone beat blind limit orders on almost every concept/timeframe (TJL H1: +19R confirmed vs -46R raw; CHOCH H1: +7R vs -16R).
- **TJL:** best were **H1 TJL1 with confirmation** (+24R / 144 trades, 29% win at 1:3) and **M15 TJL2 A+** (+28R confirmed, +73R raw). D1/H4 TJL1 were weak. The "strong impulse" filter helped H1 TJL1 (+24R) but not M15.
- **CHOCH:** positive on D1/H1 with confirmation; **SBR on H1** was the best zone (+10R, 36% win). Trades **with the higher-timeframe trend** were clearly better on M15 (+14R vs -55R against).
- **Dual CHOCH:** roughly 1 in 3 CHOCHs failed into a Dual CHOCH on M15/H1/H4 (25-34%), and most did on D1 (16 of 23). Entries from level 4 were marginal (H1 +2R, M15 -1R) and samples are small on H4/D1.
- **ISS:** rare and mostly negative overall, **except when it agreed with the higher-timeframe trend** (H1 confirmed: 3W/1L with HTF vs 0W/7L against) — same lesson as the AUDUSD example: never trade ISS against the bigger trend.
- Win rates are 20-35% at a fixed 1:3 target (break-even 25%), and price reached 1R in ~50% of confirmed trades, so partial booking at 1R-2R would change the picture — see `hit1R%`/`hit2R%`.

## Caveats
- This is an automated, rule-literal application. The course adds discretion (session timing London 11:30-14:00 / NY 17:00-20:30 IST, choosing clean structures, morning/evening star confirmations) that is not modelled; only engulfing confirmations are used.
- M15 history starts mid-Aug 2026; H1 confirmations (M5) and M15 confirmations (M1) only exist from then. D1/H4 samples are small for CHOCH, Dual CHOCH and ISS.
- No spread/commission is included.

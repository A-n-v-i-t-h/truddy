## TJL (Trend Joining Level) — from Trade Vision Fx frames
- Uptrend: impulse up to swing high = level 1 -> TJL1 = zone at that swing high (candles forming the high).
- Pullback low = level 2 -> TJL2 "A+" = zone at that higher low.
- Price breaks above level 1 -> buys on return into TJL1 (break & retest) or deeper into TJL2 (A+).
- Each new leg repeats: new TJL1 at the new broken high, new TJL2 at new higher low.
- Downtrend mirrored: TJL1 at broken swing low, TJL2 at lower high -> sells.
- Example trade shown: 1m entry, RR ~2.9.

## ISS (Internal Structure Shift / 5-wave CHOCH) — Lecture 5 P1 slide
Rules (verbatim from slide):
1. Works best in H1 & M15 timeframe.
2. Within last bullish impulse, that is from level 2 to level 3, if
3. 5 waves in opposite direction of the trend are being formed
4. in same timeframe, then we call that internal structure shift.
5. We sell from level 3 & 4 (of the 5-wave move).
6. It is indication from big players that they are ready to get into sells and structure is no longer bullish; it is bearish now.
- Labels: CH1 at the broken high, CL2 "LSM" at the low that launched the last impulse; level 3 = high of last impulse.
- After 5 waves down (1-2-3-4-5) inside the last impulse (5 still above CL2), sell zones = origin of wave-3 and wave-4 drops (red boxes), target below CL2.
- Lecture 5 P2: bearish mirror on USDJPY: downtrend 1-2-3, then 5-wave up from level 3 still below level 2 ("NORMAL CHOCH" line); buy zones at waves 3 & 4 (green boxes), targets above.
- L5P2 also shows real XAUUSD 1h replay: multiple CH1 breaks, CL2 LSM, level 3 high.

## Dual CHOCH — Dual Choc.pdf (2 slides)
Bullish -> Bearish -> Bullish:
- Uptrend legs: first high (level 1) zone = "A+"; pullback low (level 2) zone = "CHOC / SBR" (support becomes resistance after break); new high (level 3) = "double top" zone.
- NORMAL CHOCH = price CLOSES below level 2 (the last low) -> structure turns bearish.
- DUAL CHOCH = after that normal CHOCH, price reverses from an exact zone (the new low) and CLOSES above the double top (level 3) with 2 candle closings -> bullish again.
- Mark that reversal low as the new 4th level.
Bearish -> Bullish -> Bearish (mirror):
- Downtrend: first low (level 1) zone = "A+"; pullback high (level 2) = "CHOC / RBS" (resistance becomes support); new low (level 3) = "double bottom".
- NORMAL CHOCH = price CLOSES above level 2 (last high) -> bullish.
- DUAL CHOCH = price then reverses from an exact zone (the new high) and CLOSES below the double bottom with 2 candle closings -> bearish again; mark that high as new 4th level.
Key: closes, not wicks; 2-candle closing confirmation beyond level 3.

## Structure marking — STRUCTURE MARKING.pdf
Key ideas: impulse vs correction, 2-candle retracement, 1-candle closing, confirmed low & high, last support & last resistance.
Bullish structure rules:
1. Buying waves = impulse; selling waves = retracement/correction.
2. Never trade the retracement.
3. Valid retracement = 2 consecutive red candles, the second's BODY closing below the first candle's wick (low).
4. HIGH is confirmed after a 2-candle retracement (the high = top of the move before those 2 candles).
   LOW is confirmed when a candle CLOSES above that confirmed high (the low = lowest point of the retracement).
5. Don't look for a new 2-candle retracement until the previous high is broken.
6. In bullish structure the high is confirmed first.
Bearish structure rules (slide, verbatim gist): selling waves = impulse, buying waves = correction; never trade the retracement;
2 consecutive candles closing ABOVE (green, 2nd body closes above 1st's high) are valid; LOW confirmed after 2-candle retracement,
HIGH confirmed after a candle closes below that low; don't look for a new 2-candle retracement before previous low is broken; in bearish structure the low is confirmed first.

## CHOCH — CLASS NO 4 CHANGE OF CHARACTER.pdf
Bearish CHOCH (in an uptrend; levels CH1 = level 1, CL2 = level 2 = LAST SUPPORT/LSM, 3 = latest "tentative" high):
1. 2 CONSECUTIVE candles CLOSE below the last support (CL2), same timeframe. First must be red; second can be any colour but must CLOSE below last support.
2. Then look for SELLS from the last bullish structure levels, 3 levels:
   - SBR = support-becomes-resistance at level 2 (CL2 zone)
   - A+ = QML (quasimodo level) at level 1 (CH1 zone)  <- best, smallest stop loss
   - DT = double top at level 3 (the high)
3. Zone marking: for a high mark the TOP wick, for a low mark the BOTTOM wick; zone = wick + ~1% of the body.
Bullish CHOCH (mirror, in a downtrend; level 1 low, level 2 = LAST RESISTANCE, 3 = latest low):
- 2 consecutive candle closes ABOVE last resistance (first green). Then BUY from RBS (level 2), A+ (level 1, QML), DB (level 3).
Relation to TJL: TJL = continuation entries at levels 1/2 of the current trend; CHOCH = trend flipped, the same levels become counter-zones.
Dual CHOCH = a CHOCH that fails: after normal CHOCH, price closes back beyond level 3 (DT/DB) with 2 candles -> original trend resumes; reversal extreme becomes level 4.

## Internal & External structure / ISS PDF
Topics: external vs internal structure, ISS, 5-waves CHOCH, TJL, examples.
Bearish ISS slide:
1. 5 waves must be formed within the last bullish impulse, confirming the 5-wave CHOCH in the SAME timeframe.
2. Levels 3 & 4 (of the 5 waves) become selling areas after that.
3. Sign from big players that they are ready to sell; normal CHOCH takes time, so use this when seen forming.
4. Preferred TF = 1 hour; mostly in gold also 15 min.
Bullish ISS = mirror: 5 waves UP within the last bearish impulse (level 2 -> 3), same TF; levels 3 & 4 become BUYING areas.
Diagram (H1): CH1 = Confirmed High (level 1, red 2-candle retracement under it), CL2 = Confirmed Low (level 2), green candle closes above CH1 -> CL2 confirmed; impulse to 3.
"From level 2 to 3, in between level 2 to 3, if price forms 5 waves in selling structure, that is called internal structure shift."
Waves 1..5 inside are each marked with the same 2-candle retracement / closing rules (internal structure). Wave 5 stays ABOVE CL2.
Sell zones = the supply candle zone at wave 3's origin (the lower-high box) and the wave-4 high zone; targets below CL2.

Lecture 5 P1 transcript clarifications:
- Why ISS: a normal CHOCH (close below last support) can take days; ISS gives an earlier sell inside the last impulse.
- External structure = the trend (H1/H4); ISS must be on the SAME timeframe (not 5 waves on M1 against an H1 trend).
- Three conditions: opposite direction to trend, within last impulse (level 2 -> 3), same timeframe.
- 5th wave is confirmed with the normal rule: for a low, 2 GREEN candle retracement (2nd closes above 1st's high). No close below level 2 allowed (that would be a normal CHOCH instead).
- Mark level 3 and level 4 of the 5 waves (wick + 1% of body) and look for sells there.
- "We never trade against the trend." Course order: H4/daily practice first, H1/M15 from class 6 on.

## TJL slide (INTERNAL & EXTERNAL PDF)
1. TJLs show where to buy in bullish structure and where to sell in bearish structure.
2. Usually LEVEL 2 is the safest level to buy/sell, because if price closes beyond level 2 the CHOCH is confirmed (invalidation is clear).
3. ("100 pips in gold daily" claim — ignore as marketing.)
TJL diagrams:
- Bullish: level 1 = confirmed high (where the 2 red retracement candles start). TJL1 = zone at the top of level 1 (high of the first retracement candle down to ~its body). Level 2 = LAST SUPPORT (confirmed low). TJL2 (A+ SETUP) = zone at level 2 low. A green candle closes above level 1 -> impulse to level 3. Then on the pullback from 3, buy at TJL1 (retest of broken level 1) or at TJL2 (A+). Both arrows point up from those zones.
- Bearish: level 1 = confirmed low (2 green retracement candles start there) -> TJL1 zone at level 1 low. Level 2 = LAST RESISTANCE (confirmed high) -> TJL2 (A+ SETUP). Red candle closes below level 1 -> impulse to 3. Sell pullbacks into TJL1 or TJL2.
- Invalidation: close beyond level 2 = CHOCH (structure flips).
TJL entry rules (Trade Vision Fx explainer transcript):
- TJL1 = the higher high where the BOS happens; TJL2 = the higher low = last support = A+. Bearish mirror (TJL1 = lower low, TJL2 = lower high = last resistance).
- Trade TJL1 only when the BOS came with a strong impulse; if the impulse is weak / price already rejecting, skip TJL1 and wait for TJL2 A+.
- Confirmation before entry (no blind limit orders):
  - TJL on 5m/15m -> 1m engulfing or 1m morning star (evening star for sells).
  - TJL on 1H -> 5m or 1m CHOCH (or 5m engulfing / star).
- Examples: SL around the zone (~30-50 pips gold), targets ~1:3. Trade in session (London/NY).
